About Me:

My name is Shaina Saporta. I am a student at the University of Waterloo, currently in my second year in their Global Business and Digital Arts program.

My academic focus is centred on communications technology, design, and photography, and I am especially interested in how creative strategy and digital storytelling intersect within advertising. I am a dedicated and hardworking student who is eager to gain hands-on industry experience.
